How a match goes

  1. 1

    Gather.

    Twelve turns to pick fruit, chop wood and craft a club. Each ape has its own trick.

  2. 2

    The golden race.

    On turn 8 a golden fruit appears in the centre. Race for it, or keep gathering. If you both go, the ape with less fruit gets it, and nobody does on a tie.

  3. 3

    The duel.

    Strike, Guard, Feint or Eat, picked in secret and revealed together. Read your rival, break their pattern.

Fair by design

No dice in duels: secret picks decide it.

Every duel has a replay code. Same code, same game.

The apes

  • The climber.

    Only one who reaches the canopy.

  • The smasher.

    Two wood from every tree.

  • The quick one.

    Two extra turns to gather.

  • The tank.

    Every tree drops fruit too.

  • The crafter.

    Builds the club with less wood.

Real screens

One duel takes a few minutes. The rematch takes longer.
