How a match goes

Gather. Race. Duel.

  • 🍌
    1. Gather.Twelve turns to pick fruit, chop wood and craft a club. Each ape has its own trick.
  • ✨
    2. The golden race.On turn 8 a golden fruit appears in the centre. Race for it, or keep gathering. If you both go, the ape with less fruit gets it, and nobody does on a tie.
  • 🥊
    3. The duel.Strike, Guard, Feint or Eat, picked in secret and revealed together. Read your rival, break their pattern.
